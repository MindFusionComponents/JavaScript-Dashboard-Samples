Operations Dashboard

This sample showcases an interactive, real-time executive operations dashboard powered by the MindFusion JavaScript Pack:

  • MindFusion.DataViews (Grid): Virtually rendered data grid loaded with 10,000 live records. Demonstrates lightning-fast scrolling, multi-column sorting, instant search & filtering, and inline editing. Double-click any cell (Product, Category, Price, Stock, Rating, Restocked) to edit in place.
  • MindFusion.Charting (BarChart): A dynamic bar chart displaying aggregated inventory stock and valuation by product category. Automatically synchronizes with grid filtering, row selections, and cell edits.
  • MindFusion.Gauges (OvalGauge): An executive KPI gauge calculating the inventory fulfillment rate and operational stock health with color-coded warning/optimal zones.

Try searching for "Mouse" or filtering by category to see the chart and gauges adapt instantly!
